## The brand

AGENCY818 believes storytelling is more than aesthetics and trending sounds, it
is connection. Founded by actress and creator Aly Pharis, a boutique social
media and content studio helping brands show up with clarity, confidence, and a
whole lot of personality. Content strategy, scroll-stopping visuals, brand voice,
always rooted in intention. The best content does not just perform, it resonates.

For brands that want to stand out without selling out. For the ones who believe
in showing up like they mean it. For founders who want strategy and soul in
every post.

## Purpose

To help brands go from posting to performing, with content that is emotionally
resonant, visually striking, and backed by real strategy.

## Unique value proposition

Strategy meets storytelling. Founded by a working actress turned social
strategist, bringing an insider's perspective to content, leveraging camera
presence, lighting, and visual angles to create scroll-stopping content that
feels human, not manufactured.

## Values

Intentional · Approachable · Strategic · Personable · Cool, not cold

## Personality

Edgy · Approachable · Modern · Fresh

## Goals

1. **Attract premium, aligned clients.** Established brands that view social as
   strategy, not another task. Clients who respect the process, understand the
   value of intentional content, and are ready to invest without negotiating
   their way through the proposal.
2. **Build a community-driven, welcoming brand.** Warm, authentic, designed to
   foster real relationships rather than transactions. Inclusive and genuine.
3. **Expand beyond local.** Rooted in Dallas, scaling with intention to
   forward-thinking beauty, wellness, and fashion brands nationally.

## Target personas

These three are the audience. Write to them by name internally, never generically.

### 1. Vanessa Reyes · 32 · MedSpa owner, Dallas

Former RN in aesthetics who moved into entrepreneurship after years in clinical
work. Built a loyal local clientele. Wants to reach a higher-end audience and
expand into digital product offerings.

- **Goals:** attract more premium clientele, position as *the* skincare
  destination, sell virtual consults and skincare guides.
- **Challenges:** not confident on camera. Her content feels "meh" and does not
  match the in-office experience. No time to consistently plan and post.

### 2. Elle Nguyen · 27 · Founder of a Gen-Z fashion label, NYC

Fashion student turned Depop seller turned entrepreneur, now scaling her first
official collection into e-commerce. Creative, film camera everywhere, oversized
blazers. Lives on Pinterest. Solange, Emma Chamberlain.

- **Goals:** a bold, recognizable online presence. Launch the next drop with a
  real campaign. Connect with the right influencers.
- **Challenges:** overwhelmed by content creation. Burned by agencies that did
  not get it. Does not know how to translate her brand's vibe visually.

### 3. Maya James · 35 · Holistic wellness founder, supplement brand

Former yoga teacher who started sharing wellness routines online during the
pandemic, grew a small but engaged community, now has her own product line.
Nature walks, matcha, Notion. Values transparency, collaboration, and purpose
over vanity metrics, and is deeply intentional about who she hires.

- **Goals:** grow beyond lifestyle into brand-led storytelling. Build trust
  while selling more. Elevate the content.
- **Challenges:** professional shoots lose her authenticity. Visual consistency.
  Content does not sound like her, she gets stuck trying to sound professional.

## The mood

Rooted in quiet luxury and modern authority. Bold enough to lead, grounded in
approachability. Elevated and editorial without being exclusive or out of reach.
Neutral and minimal, creating space for clarity and confidence, with intentional
pops of color for energy and individuality. This brand does not gatekeep, it
guides with intention, presence, and a scroll-stopping sense of style.

Direction: high quality imagery mixed with behind-the-scenes moments, subtle
pops of color, white space, elevated but approachable.

## Chosen direction

The deck presents two options for each. AGENCY818 selected **Palette 2** and
**Typography 2**.

### Color Palette 2 (selected)

The deck names the colors without hex values. These hexes are the working set
used across the Artlist kit and the `agency818-brand` skill.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Merlot | `#471E22` | primary dark accent, headlines on light |
| Cherry | `#692022` | secondary accent, sparing emphasis |
| Slate | `#262421` | primary text, dark backgrounds |
| Plaster | `#EBE7DE` | primary light background |
| Tan | `#B7A28E` | neutral surface, muted accents |
| Pink | `#F6D7DC` | the one intentional pop, accent only |
| Base cream | `#FCFBF9` | working canvas, distinct from pure white |

*Palette 1, not selected: Bubble, Leather, Gum, Zest, Blush, Shell, Sand.*

### Typography 2 (selected)

> Different weights font. Sans serif, modern font. Accent with handwritten font.

A modern sans serif carried across multiple weights for hierarchy, with a
handwritten script used sparingly as a human accent.

*Typography 1, not selected: editorial serif font, handwritten script, mixing
italics and all caps.*

> **Known discrepancy.** The Artlist "A818 ADS" style kit's visual guidelines
> text described "an editorial high-contrast serif for display headlines," which
> is Typography 1, the direction that was **not** chosen. Corrected in the kit
> on 2026-09-09. If generated creative comes back with serif headlines, this is
> why.
